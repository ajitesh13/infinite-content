# Bhagavad Gītā ślokas for daily life

> Editorial status: **draft — requires scripture and translation review before publication**.

This is a curated reference list of Bhagavad Gītā passages with practical relevance to work, stress, relationships, habits, decision-making, self-control, and spiritual growth. The English explanations are concise editorial paraphrases, not replacements for a traditional commentary. The Sanskrit and source references should be checked by an editor before any verse is promoted into a published reel.

## Work, duty and focus

### Bhagavad Gītā 2.40 — No sincere effort is wasted

> नेहाभिक्रमनाशोऽस्ति प्रत्यवायो न विद्यते।
> स्वल्पमप्यस्य धर्मस्य त्रायते महतो भयात्॥

**Meaning:** On this path, no sincere effort is lost, and even a small amount of progress protects one from great fear.

**Daily application:** Do not underestimate small positive habits. Ten minutes of study, exercise, meditation, or self-discipline can gradually transform life.

[Source and translations](https://heritage.iitk.ac.in/SBG/2.40)

**Audio:** [2.40](../assets/audio/SBG_2_40_moolam_swmbrhm.mp3)

### Bhagavad Gītā 2.47 — Focus on your action

> कर्मण्येवाधिकारस्ते मा फलेषु कदाचन।
> मा कर्मफलहेतुर्भूर्मा ते सङ्गोऽस्त्वकर्मणि॥

**Meaning:** You have control over your actions, not complete control over their results. Do not work only for rewards, but do not become attached to laziness either.

**Daily application:** Focus on preparation, effort, honesty, and quality. Accept that promotions, praise, profits, and other results depend on many factors.

[Source and translations](https://heritage.iitk.ac.in/SBG/2.47)

**Audio:** [2.47](../assets/audio/SBG_2_47_moolam_swmbrhm.mp3)

### Bhagavad Gītā 2.48 — Maintain balance

> योगस्थः कुरु कर्माणि सङ्गं त्यक्त्वा धनञ्जय।
> सिद्ध्यसिद्ध्योः समो भूत्वा समत्वं योग उच्यते॥

**Meaning:** Perform your duties while remaining balanced in success and failure. This inner balance is called yoga.

**Daily application:** Celebrate success without arrogance and accept failure without collapsing emotionally.

[Source and translations](https://heritage.iitk.ac.in/SBG/2.48)

### Bhagavad Gītā 2.50 — Excellence through inner balance

> बुद्धियुक्तो जहातीह उभे सुकृतदुष्कृते।
> तस्माद्योगाय युज्यस्व योगः कर्मसु कौशलम्॥

**Meaning:** A person joined to wisdom rises above attachment to both success and failure. Yoga is skillfulness in action.

**Daily application:** True excellence includes clear thinking, ethical conduct, emotional stability, and learning from results—not merely achieving an outcome.

### Bhagavad Gītā 3.5 — Nobody can remain inactive

> न हि कश्चित्क्षणमपि जातु तिष्ठत्यकर्मकृत्।
> कार्यते ह्यवशः कर्म सर्वः प्रकृतिजैर्गुणैः॥

**Meaning:** Nobody can remain without action even for a moment. Everyone is compelled to act by nature and circumstances.

**Daily application:** If you do not consciously choose your actions, habits, impulses, and external influences will choose for you.

### Bhagavad Gītā 3.8 — Perform your responsibilities

> शरीरयात्रापि च ते न प्रसिद्ध्येदकर्मणः॥

**Meaning:** Perform your prescribed duties; action is better than inaction. Even the maintenance of the body is impossible without action.

**Daily application:** Take care of basic responsibilities—health, family, work, finances, and commitments—rather than escaping from them.

### Bhagavad Gītā 3.30 — Work without anxiety and possessiveness

**Meaning:** Dedicate all actions to the Divine, give up selfish expectation and possessiveness, and perform your duty without inner fever or anxiety.

**Daily application:** Before a difficult task, remind yourself: “I will give my best effort, but I will not allow fear of the result to control me.”

**Daily application:** Do not constantly compare your career, personality, or life with others. Understand your own abilities, responsibilities, and values. This should be understood as one’s rightful responsibility and aptitude—not as a justification for social discrimination.

### Bhagavad Gītā 18.45–46 — Make your work meaningful

> स्वे स्वे कर्मण्यभिरतः संसिद्धिं लभते नरः।
> स्वकर्मनिरतः सिद्धिं यथा विन्दति तच्छृणु॥
>
> यतः प्रवृत्तिर्भूतानां येन सर्वमिदं ततम्।
> स्वकर्मणा तमभ्यर्च्य सिद्धिं विन्दति मानवः॥

**Meaning:** A person can attain fulfilment by sincerely performing their own work and dedicating it to the source of all life.

**Daily application:** Even ordinary work can become meaningful when performed with care, responsibility, and service-mindedness.

## Managing stress, desire and anger

### Bhagavad Gītā 2.14 — Difficult experiences are temporary

> मात्रास्पर्शास्तु कौन्तेय शीतोष्णसुखदुःखदाः।
> आगमापायिनोऽनित्यास्तांस्तितिक्षस्व भारत॥

**Meaning:** Pleasure and pain, heat and cold, arise through contact with the world; they come and go because they are temporary. Endure them patiently.

**Daily application:** Do not make permanent decisions based on temporary sadness, anger, discomfort, or excitement.

[Source and translations](https://heritage.iitk.ac.in/SBG/2.14)

### Bhagavad Gītā 2.16 — Distinguish the temporary from the lasting

> नासतो विद्यते भावो नाभावो विद्यते सतः।
> उभयोरपि दृष्टोऽन्तस्त्वनयोस्तत्त्वदर्शिभिः॥

**Meaning:** The temporary has no lasting existence, while the enduring does not cease to exist. The wise distinguish between the two.

**Daily application:** Fame, money, opinions, moods, possessions, and problems change. Give greater importance to character, truth, relationships, and inner stability.

[Source and translations](https://heritage.iitk.ac.in/SBG/2.16)

### Bhagavad Gītā 2.22 — Change is part of life

> वासांसि जीर्णानि यथा विहाय
> नवानि गृह्णाति नरोऽपराणि।
> तथा शरीराणि विहाय जीर्णा-
> न्यन्यानि संयाति नवानि देही॥

**Meaning:** Just as a person discards old clothes and puts on new ones, the embodied self passes from one body to another.

**Daily application:** Use this teaching to face ageing, change, loss, and new beginnings with greater acceptance. It does not mean that grief should be suppressed or that people should be treated without compassion.

### Bhagavad Gītā 2.62–63 — The chain that leads to anger

> ध्यायतो विषयान् पुंसः सङ्गस्तेषूपजायते।
> सङ्गात्सञ्जायते कामः कामात्क्रोधोऽभिजायते॥
>
> क्रोधाद्भवति संमोहः संमोहात्स्मृतिविभ्रमः।
> स्मृतिभ्रंशाद् बुद्धिनाशो बुद्धिनाशात्प्रणश्यति॥

**Meaning:** Repeatedly thinking about an object creates attachment; attachment creates desire; frustrated desire creates anger. Anger leads to confusion, loss of memory and judgement, and eventually self-destruction.

**Daily application:** Interrupt the chain early. Reduce obsessive scrolling, comparison, fantasy, and repeated attention to things that trigger unhealthy craving.

### Bhagavad Gītā 2.64–65 — Self-control produces clarity

> रागद्वेषवियुक्तैस्तु विषयानिन्द्रियैश्चरन्।

**Meaning:** One who moves through the world without being controlled by attraction and hatred gains inner serenity. From serenity comes clarity and relief from suffering.

**Daily application:** The goal is not necessarily to avoid everything enjoyable. It is to enjoy without becoming dependent or enslaved.

### Bhagavad Gītā 2.70–71 — Become like the ocean

## Self-discipline, empathy and relationships

### Bhagavad Gītā 6.5–6 — Your mind can be your friend or enemy

> उद्धरेदात्मनात्मानं नात्मानमवसादयेत्।

**Meaning:** A person should lift themselves up, not bring themselves down. The disciplined mind becomes a friend; the uncontrolled mind behaves like an enemy.

**Daily application:** Your self-talk, habits, choices, and use of time can either strengthen or weaken you.

### Bhagavad Gītā 18.33 — Develop steady determination

> धृत्या यया धारयते मनःप्राणेन्द्रियक्रियाः।
> योगेनाव्यभिचारिण्या धृतिः सा पार्थ सात्त्विकी॥

**Meaning:** Steady determination is the strength by which a person holds the mind, vital energy, senses, and actions firmly to a disciplined path.

**Daily application:** Motivation changes daily; disciplined commitment keeps you moving when motivation disappears.

### Bhagavad Gītā 18.63 — Think deeply and choose responsibly

> विमृश्यैतदशेषेण यथेच्छसि तथा कुरु॥

### Bhagavad Gītā 18.66 — Surrender fear and ego

> सर्वधर्मान्परित्यज्य मामेकं शरणं व्रज।
> अहं त्वां सर्वपापेभ्यो मोक्षयिष्यामि मा शुचः॥

**Meaning:** Take refuge in the Divine alone; I will free you from all wrongdoing—do not grieve.

**Daily application:** This does not mean abandoning moral duties or responsibilities. It means surrendering ego, fear, guilt, and confusion to a higher truth while continuing to act rightly.

### Bhagavad Gītā 18.78 — Wisdom and effort together

> यत्र योगेश्वरः कृष्णो यत्र पार्थो धनुर्धरः।
> तत्र श्रीर्विजयो भूतिर्ध्रुवा नीतिर्मतिर्मम॥

**Meaning:** Wherever there is wisdom and guidance together with committed effort and courage, there will be prosperity, success, strength, and righteousness.

**Daily application:** Good intentions alone are insufficient; effort alone can become directionless. Combine ethical wisdom with disciplined execution.
